FAQ

Before you join

Can I join from outside Ireland?

The core experience is virtual, so location need not be a barrier. Each cohort must show a regional evening time before booking; one live time will not suit every time zone.

Is the wine-bar meetup part of the six weeks?

The six virtual meetings are the core term. A week-six in-person social is optional and offered only where a venue and event have been announced. Travel, drink, tapas, merchandise and any event price must be disclosed separately before booking.

Will I meet the author?

An author-curated reading list is the programme concept. An author appearance is a separate, event-specific opportunity, not a promise for every cohort or location.

What will it cost, and does it renew?

The working price range is €60–€120 for six virtual meetings; the final cohort price, inclusions and any renewal terms are not yet set. This prototype does not take payment or reserve a place.
